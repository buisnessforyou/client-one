Our Philosophy

Rest is not a luxury —

Every piece is made from responsibly sourced materials, crafted with the care of artisans who believe what touches your skin should be as good for the earth as it is for you.

The Craft

Materials That Matter

We choose every fibre, every thread, every ingredient with intention.

Organic Cotton

GOTS-certified, grown without synthetic pesticides. Softer on skin, lighter on the earth.

Bamboo

Naturally antimicrobial, grown without irrigation or pesticides. The world's most renewable textile.

Recycled Fibres

Our fleece is made from post-consumer plastic bottles — each blanket repurposes up to 50 bottles.

Soy Wax

100% natural, sustainably grown soy. Burns cleaner and longer than paraffin — better air, better home.
